# User Guide — Thai Customs Classification Assistant

An AI service that answers questions about Thai customs classification (HS Codes), import duty rates, FTA privileges, and related documents for importing goods into Thailand.

Website: https://gateway-1008099094873.asia-southeast1.run.app

## 1. Getting started

1. Open the website above in a browser (latest Chrome or Safari recommended).
2. Sign in using one of these methods:
   - "Login with Google" button — fastest, no password to remember.
   - Enter your email + password and press "Login" (press "Register" first if you don't have an account yet).
3. New users receive 30 free trial credits (3 questions).

## 2. Data consent (one time only)

Before your first question, the system asks you to tick "consent to store questions and answers to improve the service" and press confirm. You only do this once.

## 3. Asking questions

1. Type your question in the box at the bottom, e.g. "How much duty for importing Bluetooth speakers from China?"
2. Press Enter or the "Send" button (Shift+Enter = new line).
3. Wait for the AI answer (normally within 1-2 minutes). Each question costs 10 credits.
4. You can keep asking follow-up questions on the same topic — the system remembers the conversation context.

Tip: the more product detail you give (product type, materials, country of origin), the more accurate the answer.

## 4. Attaching files to a question

Press the "Attach file" button before sending. Supported files (max 20MB):

- Images (JPG/PNG etc.) — e.g. product photos, labels, brochures; the AI can read images.
- Spreadsheets (.xlsx/.xls) — e.g. product lists.
- Documents (.pdf/.docx) — e.g. invoices, spec sheets.
- Text (.csv/.txt) — e.g. data lists.

## 5. Credits and top-up

- 1 question = 10 credits (including questions with attachments).
- Your remaining balance is shown in the top bar.
- Out of credits: click a package button in the "Top up" box and pay by credit/debit card via Stripe (secure — we never store your card number). Credits are added immediately after payment.

Packages:

- 100 credits — 100 THB
- 500 credits — 450 THB
- 1,000 credits — 800 THB

## 6. FAQ

Credits missing after payment? — Wait a moment and refresh the page. If they still don't appear, contact us with the email you registered with.

"insufficient_credits" error? — Your balance is below 10; please top up first.

"too fast, wait a moment" error? — You sent questions too quickly; wait about 20 seconds and resend.

Can't attach a file? — Check the file type and size (max 20MB).

Forgot your password? — If you registered with email, contact us for a password reset. If you use Google login, just sign in with Google again.
