1. ข้อมูลที่แอพเก็บ

  • ไฟล์แนบ (รูป/เอกสาร): ประมวลผลในหน่วยความจำเพื่อตอบคำถามแล้วทิ้งทันที ไม่เก็บเนื้อหาไฟล์ไว้ (เก็บเฉพาะชื่อไฟล์ ขนาด และชนิดไฟล์)

Privacy Policy — ThaiCustoms App (English)

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Data we handle

  • Questions and answers: stored as text on our server to show your history and improve accuracy.
  • Attached files (photos/documents): processed in memory to answer your question, then discarded immediately. File contents are not stored (only file name, size, and type are kept).
  • Account: Firebase account (with email only if you sign in with email or Google); credit balance, usage history, and consent are stored on Firebase (asia-southeast1) to operate the service.
  • In-app top-ups: billed through Google Play, with Google as the payment processor. We receive only the order ID and product to grant credits and never see your card details.

2. Purpose and sharing

Data is used solely to answer HS-code, duty, and FTA questions. We do not sell data or serve ads. Processors: Google Cloud infrastructure (Firebase/Firestore/Vertex AI), Google Play (in-app top-ups only), and Stripe (website top-ups only).

3. Your rights

Request access, correction, deletion, or portability at yaw2558@gmail.com. Uninstalling removes on-device data; request server-side deletion via the same email (see the deletion page).

4. Children

ThaiCustoms is a general-audience business tool, not specifically directed at children under 13.
